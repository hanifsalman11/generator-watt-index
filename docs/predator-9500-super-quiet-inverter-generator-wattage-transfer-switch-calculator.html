Frequently Asked Questions (Predator 9500 Super Quiet Inverter Generator)

Can the Predator 9500 Super Quiet Inverter Generator run a 3-Ton or 4-Ton central air conditioner?

A 3-Ton central AC uses ~3,200 running watts but demands 8,500W–11,000W of starting surge (LRA). With 9,500 peak watts (7,600W running), installing a Soft Starter on the AC condenser drops startup surge to ~3,600W, allowing the Predator 9500 Super Quiet Inverter Generator to run your central AC, well pump, and refrigerators simultaneously.

How much fuel does the Predator 9500 Super Quiet Inverter Generator burn per hour and per day?

At a typical 50% household load, it consumes ~0.55 gallons/hour of gasoline (~13.2 gallons per 24 hours) or ~0.67 gallons/hour of liquid propane (lasting ~7.0 hours on a 20-lb tank or ~35.0 hours on a 100-lb propane tank).

Do I need a 30-Amp or 50-Amp inlet box and cord for the Predator 9500 Super Quiet Inverter Generator?

A 30-Amp NEMA L14-30 circuit maxes out at 7,200 Watts (30A × 240V), whereas a 50-Amp CS6364/SS2-50 circuit handles up to 12,000 Watts (50A × 240V). For the Predator 9500 Super Quiet Inverter Generator, use a 30-Amp inlet box wired with 10/4 AWG (30-Amp SJTW/STW Pure Copper Cord).
